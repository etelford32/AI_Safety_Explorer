Why blinded

If the annotator can see that a response came from the variant labelled "explicitly harmful", they will score it as degraded, and the instrument measures the annotator's expectations rather than the model's behaviour. Metadata is revealed only after the rating is submitted, and the reveal is logged. Unblinded annotations are still stored — they are just reported separately and are not the reference set.
